<section>
  <div class="container-narrow">
    <span class="eyebrow">Frequently asked</span>
    <h2 style="margin-top:16px;">What landowners usually ask us</h2>
    <div class="faq" style="margin-top:40px;">
      <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>How do I sell land near a power line or substation in Texas?</h3>
        <p>Start by telling us where the land is and what you know about nearby transmission infrastructure — you don't need to know the voltage or ownership. We do that research ourselves using ERCOT, utility, and county records, then tell you honestly whether it's a fit and what it might be worth.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>Do I need a realtor to sell my land to a data center developer?</h3>
        <p>No. We buy directly from landowners and brokers alike. If you already have a broker, we're glad to work with them; if you don't, you don't need to hire one to talk to us.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>What makes land valuable for a data center site?</h3>
        <p>Proximity to a substation or transmission line is the headline, but the real driver is whether that substation has spare capacity to accept new load — most nearby substations in Texas don't. Parcel size, shape, flood zone, water access, and zoning also matter. We screen for all of it before making an offer.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>How fast can you close?</h3>
        <p>Once we've confirmed the site fits, terms are usually a short paid option period (60–180 days) to finish utility and title diligence, followed by a cash closing. We move as fast as the utility and title work allow — often faster than a traditional buyer financing a purchase.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>My land has multiple owners, or is in probate — can you still buy it?</h3>
        <p>Yes. We regularly work with heirship property, estates in probate, and land held across several family members. Tell us the situation up front and we'll structure around it.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>Is there a minimum acreage?</h3>
        <p>We typically look at parcels of 20 acres or more, but smaller tracts can matter if they sit next to a larger assemblage we're already evaluating. When in doubt, send it to us — the screen is free.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>Do you pay cash, and is there a fee to talk to you?</h3>
        <p>Yes, closings are cash. There's no cost or obligation to have us screen your land — most of what we check is public record, and we'll tell you plainly if it isn't a fit.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
